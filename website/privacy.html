Privacy Policy - PocketLion26 Games

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This policy covers the free, ad-supported games published by PocketLion26, including Arcade Ascent (each one, "the app"). It explains what is and isn't collected.

What stays on your device

Your progress (coins, upgrades, settings, high scores) is saved on your device only. We do not run a server and cannot see it.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app may collect and use:

  • device identifiers (including the advertising identifier, only if you allow it),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • ad interaction and app usage data,
  • crash and performance data.

Google uses this to deliver ads, limit how often you see them, measure performance, prevent fraud and keep the SDK working. We do not receive your personal information from Google and do not link this data to your identity.

Learn more: how Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchases (in games that offer them)

Some of our games sell in-game currency or extra content. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your card or payment details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed so the item can be unlocked, and your purchase can be restored on your devices through your Apple account. Games that sell nothing (such as Arcade Ascent) have no purchases at all.

Your choices

  • Tracking permission (iOS). On first launch the game asks whether it may

track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you tap "Ask App not to Track", you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised using your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings -> Privacy & Security -> Tracking.

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland). Where required by law you will be asked

for consent before ads are personalised. You can review or change it at any time in the game under Settings -> Ad Privacy Options.

  • Limit ad personalisation on iOS in Settings -> Privacy & Security -> Apple Advertising.

What we do not collect

No accounts, no name or email, no contacts, no photos, no microphone, no precise location. We do not run any analytics other than the ad SDK described above.

Save codes

The optional Export feature creates a text code of your progress that you can copy yourself. It is only shared if you choose to share it.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.
